# Stock News

**English** · [Dansk](Home-da) · [Bahasa Melayu](Home-ms)

A small Python program for Day 36 of Udemy's "100 Days of Code". It compares the last two closing prices of one stock and, when the price moved by 5 percent or more, sends the three latest news articles about the company with `pywhatkit`.
